Sketchlight: AR Drawing Sketch

Trace it, then draw it yourself.

Sketchlight shows a drawing over your camera view so you can trace it onto real paper, or lights up the screen so you can trace through thin paper like a lightbox. Tips for tracing

  • Put the phone on a glass, a stack of books or a phone stand about 20-30 cm above the paper, then use Lock so a stray touch does not move the drawing.
  • The drawing shakes or blurs? Tap the paper on screen to focus, then lock focus. Good light helps more than zoom.
  • No stand? Use Screen mode: lay thin paper on the phone and trace through it.

Subscriptions

Ad privacy choices: Settings > Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
