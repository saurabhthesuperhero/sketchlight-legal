Privacy Policy: Sketchlight

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Sketchlight: AR Drawing Sketch ("the app", package com.saikaizen.sketchlight) helps you trace and learn to draw. It is published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). This page says exactly what the app does with data. There is no account and no server of ours: we never receive your photos, drawings or videos.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera: the camera picture is used live on your phone to show the template over your paper, and to take a photo or record a video of your drawing when you press the button. Camera images are processed on the device only and are never uploaded. The app does not record sound (no microphone permission).
  • Photos you import: when you choose "Photo to sketch", Android's photo picker gives the app only the one picture you pick. The line sketch is made on your phone. The photo is never uploaded.
  • Your drawings, photos and videos made in the app, your favorites, progress, streak, stars, badges and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself with the Android share sheet. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone (you can turn them off in Settings or Android settings). Nothing is sent to a server to schedule them.

What is sent over the internet, and to whom

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google's User Messaging Platform and ad partners (free version only). To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first, Google receives your phone's advertising ID, IP address (which shows your approximate location), app and device information and how you interact with ads. AdMob may let these mediation partners bid to show an ad, and they receive the same kind of data under their own policies: Meta Audience Network (Meta privacy policy) and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) (Liftoff privacy policy). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads), and change your consent choice in the app: Settings > Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Sketchlight Pro removes all ads.

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. The app sends usage events (for example: a template was opened, a drawing was finished, which mode was used, a paywall or ad was shown, a purchase or trial started; never the picture itself) with device information (phone model, Android version, language, country), an app instance identifier and approximate location derived from your IP address. Crash reports contain the phone model, Android version and the technical state of the app at the crash. None of this contains your name, e-mail, photos or drawings. Processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

Google Play Billing, only if you buy Sketchlight Pro. Payments, the free trial and renewals are handled by Google Play. The app only learns which plan is active; we never see your card or payment details.

Google Play In-App Review and In-App Updates show Google Play's own rating and update windows. Updates are never forced: you can always close the update window.

We do not sell your data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) when it goes to these services.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: Android settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy choices in the app where shown.
  • Reminders: Settings in the app, or Android notification settings.
  • Delete everything the app stored: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. We hold no data of yours that could be deleted on request; analytics and crash data are kept by Google for its standard retention period and are not linked to your identity.
